How to read this catalog

“Pages available” means the listed screens rendered in at least one recorded check. The 10 November 2026 desktop audit found rendered content or forms at 468 distinct paths, mostly as an administrator. This historical count is not a list of passed workflows, and those paths were not all retested on build 488.

Selected desktop and phone checks on 21 November 2026 UTC used a local SQLite preview and a separate preserved-data test copy. They checked representative reads, normal sign-in/out, access boundaries and one matching attachment download. They do not certify every action, database, browser or Redmine version. The action and gap columns keep those limits beside the screens.

For dated detail, read Progress and the Journal activity timeline. See existing issue-list, project-members and Wiki screenshots; their original dates and captions still apply.
