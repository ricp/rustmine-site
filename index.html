Why I started

I love Redmine. I also want it to be faster.

I’ve used Redmine since 2007, including for customers. I’m building RustMine to see whether Rust can preserve the data and behavior those projects depend on while using fewer resources. I haven’t measured a speed or cost improvement yet. Read the full story.
