Design exploration

Three possible identities. None chosen yet.

The design agent developed these proposals. I have not chosen one or applied one to the product.

01 / Technical momentum

Foundry

A bold, technical direction built from dark carbon, chalk, and a bright ember accent.

Colors: carbon, ember, chalk, slate, signal.

Type proposal: Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono.

02 / Operational clarity

Clearline

A calm, organized direction with deep navy, action blue, and light open space.

Colors: deep navy, action blue, path green, cool mist, canvas.

Type proposal: Manrope and IBM Plex Mono.

03 / Human collaboration

Common Ground

A warmer direction that pairs forest green and clay with soft paper tones.

Colors: forest, clay, warm paper, sage, earth ink.

Type proposal: Fraunces and DM Sans.

These are identity concepts: real marks, palette ideas, and type choices. They are not screens from a finished RustMine interface.
